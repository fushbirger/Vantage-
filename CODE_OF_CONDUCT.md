# Code of Conduct
## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Vantage community a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful experience for everyone.

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for all contributors, regardless of age, background, experience level, nationality, gender identity, or technical expertise.

We believe that collaboration, mutual respect, and constructive communication are essential to building a better open-source project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and welcoming.
- Using inclusive and professional language.
- Providing constructive feedback and accepting criticism gracefully.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and approaches.
- Helping newcomers understand the project and its development process.
- Focusing on what is best for the Vantage community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate intimidation or threatening behavior.
- Spam, malicious contributions, or intentionally disruptive activity.
- Any form of inappropriate conduct in project discussions or communication channels.
- Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces related to Vantage, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Code reviews and contributions.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where individuals represent the Vantage project.
- Reporting Issues

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

You can contact the maintainers privately through GitHub or use the contact information provided in the repository.

Please include as much relevant information as possible while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private or sensitive information.

All reports will be reviewed seriously and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review and investigate reports of unacceptable behavior.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

- A warning.
- Temporary suspension from project discussions or contributions.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary or permanent exclusion from the project.

Maintainers are expected to act fairly, consistently, and proportionately.
